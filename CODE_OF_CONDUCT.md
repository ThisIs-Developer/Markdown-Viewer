# Code of Conduct

Markdown Viewer welcomes people of every background and experience level. This is the single Code of Conduct for the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces, including public interactions when representing the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat people with respect, patience, and empathy.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect different experiences, perspectives, identities, and accessibility needs.
- Keep discussions relevant and help others participate.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing someone's private information without consent are not allowed. Neither are spam, sustained disruption, or retaliation against someone for reporting a concern.

## Reporting Concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, Baivab Sarkar, at [baivabsarkar@gmail.com](mailto:baivabsarkar@gmail.com). Include relevant links, context, and the behavior you experienced or observed. Share only the personal information needed to understand the report.

The maintainer will review reports fairly and limit disclosure of personal details to what is necessary to address the concern. Response times may vary with maintainer availability.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request a correction, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently ban someone from project spaces. The response depends on the severity, impact, and any repeated behavior; serious misconduct can result in an immediate ban.

To request reconsideration, reply privately with relevant context. Do not contact or pressure the reporter.

Technical vulnerability reports follow the separate [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).
